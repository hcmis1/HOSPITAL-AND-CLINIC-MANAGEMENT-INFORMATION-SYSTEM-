Monthly facility summary report

Compiles the statistics HCMIS already tracks, organised under the general section headings used in Uganda's HMIS 105/108 monthly returns (OPD attendance, diagnoses, immunizations, HIV testing, lab tests, admissions/deliveries, financial summary). This is not a verified exact replica of the official form — cross-check row order and labels against your current HMIS 105/108 template or DHIS2 entry screen before submitting. A few lines the official form asks for (antenatal care visits, family planning) aren't tracked as distinct HCMIS modules yet, and are marked as such below.
